# spec: Windscreen

## Product Type

Windscreen — laminated front glazing of a passenger car. It forms the transparent front of the cabin, keeps wind and weather out, and carries a bonded interface to the body.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car.

## Function

- Forward visibility for the driver (transparency, undistorted view of the road and of signals)
- Occupant protection when the glass breaks (fragments held together, head impact)
- Resistance to stone impact, scratching, heat, moisture and sunlight
- Bonded and sealed mounting to the body
- Part marking and traceability

## Main Parts

- Outer glass ply
- Inner glass ply
- Polyvinyl-butyral (PVB) interlayer
- Black ceramic print (obscuration band) around the edge
- Edge primer zone for the bonding adhesive

## Materials

Representative values — confirm against actual bill of materials. Total nominal thickness 4.96 mm (2.1 mm + 0.76 mm + 2.1 mm).


| Component        | Typical material(s)                          |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Glass plies      | Soda-lime float glass, 2.1 mm each, colourless |
| Interlayer       | PVB film, 0.76 mm, colourless                |
| Obscuration band | Ceramic enamel (black frit), fired on        |
| Edge primer      | Polyurethane primer for the bonding adhesive |
