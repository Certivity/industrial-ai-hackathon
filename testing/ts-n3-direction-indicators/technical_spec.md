# spec: Direction Indicator and Hazard Warning Lamp System for Heavy Truck (N3)

## Product Type

A combined direction indicator and hazard warning lamp system for heavy trucks, consisting of front, side and rear indicator lamps with a hazard warning function.

## Vehicle Category

N3 (heavy truck), fitted as original equipment on the vehicle.

## Function

- Indicates the intended direction of travel by flashing front, side and rear lamps on the selected side.
- Provides a hazard warning by flashing all direction indicator lamps simultaneously.
- Operates with a defined flash rate and duty cycle.
- Is visible from the required angles and distances.

## Main Parts

- Front direction indicator lamps (left and right).
- Side direction indicator repeaters (left and right).
- Rear direction indicator lamps (left and right).
- Flasher/control unit with hazard warning switch.
- Wiring harness and connectors.
