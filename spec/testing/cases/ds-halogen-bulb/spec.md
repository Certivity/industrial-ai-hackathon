# spec: H7 halogen filament lamp (PX26d cap)

## Product Type

A replaceable H7 halogen filament lamp with a PX26d axial cap, producing the low-beam or high-beam light from a motor vehicle headlamp. It is a filament light source, not a complete headlamp.

## Vehicle Category

M1 and N1; fitted into the headlamp of a passenger car or light commercial vehicle to give forward road illumination.

## Function

- Emit a defined quantity of white light from a single axial filament inside a halogen-filled glass bulb.
- Provide a controlled beam when placed in a headlamp reflector or projector designed for an H7 source.
- Be replaceable without tools through the PX26d bayonet cap in the headlamp socket.

## Main Parts

- Glass bulb (quartz/UV-cut glass) with the integral PX26d cap and locating keys.
- Halogen filament (tungsten coil) with support wires, mounted axially.
- Cap contacts and terminals inside the PX26d cap, sealed to the bulb.
- Halogen gas fill (inert gas with a trace of halogen).
