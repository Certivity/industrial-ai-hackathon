# spec: Replacement Driver Airbag Module (Inflator and Cover)

## Product Type

A complete replacement driver airbag module — folded airbag cushion, pyrotechnic inflator, and horn/cover pad — supplied as an aftermarket part for mounting in the steering wheel hub of a passenger car. It deploys to cushion the driver's head and chest in a frontal impact.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to the driver's seating position of a passenger car built with a steering-wheel-integrated driver airbag and a matching clock-spring/control-unit interface.

## Function

- Rapidly inflate the driver airbag cushion in a frontal or near-frontal crash to restrain the driver's head and torso.
- Absorb and dissipate occupant kinetic energy through cushion venting and controlled deflation.
- Provide the steering-wheel centre cover and horn-pad surface that splits along a defined tear seam during deployment.
- Interface electrically with the vehicle's airbag control unit and diagnostics through the existing squib connector.
- Maintain the steering-wheel appearance and horn actuation in normal non-deployment use.

## Main Parts

- Folded airbag cushion with tethers and vent holes.
- Pyrotechnic inflator (gas generator) with squib and electrical connector.
- Module housing and retainer ring, with mounting studs to the steering-wheel armature.
- Cover/horn pad with moulded tear seam and emblem insert.
- Wiring harness pigtail and connector keying.
