# spec: Adaptive Front Lighting System with Cornering Function

## Product Type

A headlamp system that automatically adapts the beam pattern and swivels the low-beam light distribution with steering angle and vehicle speed to illuminate the road through curves. It also provides a static or dynamic cornering light that widens illumination on the inside of a turn at low speed.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with a 12 V electrical system and a steering-angle sensor on the steering column.

## Function

- Projects a passing (low) beam whose horizontal cut-off and light distribution change with steering angle and vehicle speed.
- Swivels the headlamp modules (dynamic bending light) to follow the road curvature.
- Switches on an additional cornering lamp at low speed and large steering angle to light the inside of the turn.
- Provides a class-specific town, country and motorway light distribution selected automatically by speed.
- Performs self-levelling and diagnostics, and fails safe to a fixed low beam if a fault is detected.
- Communicates over the vehicle CAN bus with the steering, speed and body control units.

## Main Parts

- Left and right headlamp housings with projector or reflector modules.
- LED or gas-discharge light sources and electronic control gear (ballast/driver).
- Swivel actuators (stepper or DC motors) with position feedback.
- Static cornering lamp modules in each headlamp.
- Headlamp control unit (AFS ECU) with CAN interface and diagnostic memory.
- Levelling sensors on front and rear axle.
- Steering-angle and vehicle-speed signals from existing vehicle sensors.
