# spec: Temporary-use spare wheel and tyre unit for N1 vehicles

## Product Type

A compact temporary-use spare wheel and tyre assembly supplied with a light van, intended to replace a damaged road wheel for a limited distance and at reduced speed until the vehicle can be repaired.

## Vehicle Category

N1 — light goods vehicle; the unit is carried on board and used only in an emergency.

## Function

- Provides a serviceable wheel to replace a punctured or damaged road wheel.
- Allows the vehicle to be driven at limited speed and distance to a repair facility.
- Stows compactly to save cargo space and weight compared with a full-size spare.

## Main Parts

- Wheel rim (temporary-use type, steel or light alloy)
- Tyre (temporary-use, with reduced section width and speed rating)
- Valve and valve cap
- Wheel fixing bolts/nuts (if specific to the temporary wheel)
- Instruction label and warning markings
