# spec: Laminated Safety Windscreen and Side Glazing (M1)

## Product Type

A laminated safety-glass windscreen and tempered or laminated side glazing set for a passenger car. It provides an impact-resistant, optically clear barrier that keeps occupants inside and protects them from glass shards and external objects.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car with up to eight seats besides the driver.

## Function

- Provide a clear, distortion-free forward and side view for the driver and occupants.
- Resist penetration from external impacts and retain occupants during a collision.
- Prevent dangerous glass fragmentation by holding shards in a bonding interlayer or by controlled breakage into small dice.
- Reduce injury risk on occupant contact with the glazing.
- Maintain a safe residual view through the windscreen after damage.

## Main Parts

- Laminated windscreen assembly (two glass plies with a plastic interlayer).
- Tempered side-window glass panes.
- Bonding/interlayer film for the laminated pane.
- Edge seal and peripheral frit/black-print band.
- Fixing and sealing adhesives and trim gaskets.
