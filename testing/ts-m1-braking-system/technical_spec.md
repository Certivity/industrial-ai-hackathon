# spec: Integrated Electronic Stability and Service/Parking Brake System for M1 Vehicles

## Product Type

An integrated brake system that provides service braking, parking braking, and electronic stability control for passenger cars. It combines hydraulic modulation, electronic control, and sensors to ensure vehicle stability and braking performance.

## Vehicle Category

M1, fitted to passenger cars with up to eight seats in addition to the driver's seat.

## Function

- Provides service braking for all four wheels with anti-lock braking (ABS) and electronic brake-force distribution (EBD).
- Provides parking braking via an electronic parking brake (EPB) acting on the rear wheels.
- Maintains vehicle stability through electronic stability control (ESC), including yaw control and traction control.
- Integrates with other vehicle systems via a controller area network (CAN) for data exchange and diagnostics.

## Main Parts

- Hydraulic modulator unit with pump and valves.
- Electronic control unit (ECU) with embedded software.
- Wheel speed sensors (four).
- Steering angle sensor.
- Yaw rate and lateral acceleration sensor.
- Electronic parking brake (EPB) calipers with electric motors.
- Brake pedal travel sensor.
- Wiring harness and connectors.
