# spec: Retro-reflective Markings and Rear Retro-Reflectors for Heavy Goods Trailers (O3/O4)

## Product Type

A set of retro-reflective marking panels, strips and rear retro-reflectors for installation on heavy goods trailers of categories O3 and O4, used to render the trailer conspicuous to other road users in the dark when illuminated by headlamps.

## Vehicle Category

O3 and O4 (heavy goods trailers), fitted to the rear and sides of the trailer and to its rear underrun protection where applicable.

## Function

- Provide bright, coloured retro-reflective marking of the trailer's rear and side contours.
- Indicate the presence, width and length of the trailer to following and crossing traffic.
- Serve as rear retro-reflectors (red) marking the rear extremities.
- Remain effective when the trailer is parked or unlit.

## Main Parts

- Retro-reflective marking panels (rear and side).
- Rear retro-reflectors (red, triangular or rectangular as applicable).
- Mounting brackets, adhesive or rivet fixings.
- Protective housing or backing plates where required.
