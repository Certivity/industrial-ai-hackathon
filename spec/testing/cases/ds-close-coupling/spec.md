# spec: Drawbar Eye Close-Coupling Device for N3 Goods Vehicle Combination

## Product Type

A mechanical close-coupling device with an integrated drawbar eye that attaches a drawbar trailer to the rear of a rigid N3 goods vehicle, forming a short, tight-coupled combination.

## Vehicle Category

The device is fitted to a category N3 goods vehicle (rigid lorry over 12 t GVW) and couples it to a trailer whose drawbar eye it receives; the combination operates on public roads.

## Function

- Transfers longitudinal traction and braking forces between the towing vehicle and the trailer.
- Holds the trailer at a fixed, short coupling distance to reduce overall combination length and yaw swing.
- Provides controlled articulation in pitch and yaw at the eye-and-hook interface.
- Carries vertical drawbar load (nose weight) into the vehicle chassis.
- Allows quick, secure coupling and uncoupling with a positive-lock indication.

## Main Parts

- Coupling body with mounting flange bolted to the vehicle rear cross-member.
- Forged or cast coupling hook/jaw with positive latch and secondary safety lock.
- Drawbar eye (ring) with replaceable wear bush.
- Pivot and bearing set allowing yaw rotation.
- Latch release handle with visual lock-state indicator.
- Mounting bolts, spacers and reinforcement plate.
- Wear liners and grease points.
