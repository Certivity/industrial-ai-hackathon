# spec: LPG Fuel System Conversion Kit for Petrol Passenger Cars

## Product Type

A retrofit kit that converts a petrol-fuelled passenger car to bi-fuel operation, adding a liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) supply, vaporisation, metering and electronic control system that lets the engine run on LPG while retaining the original petrol system.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car originally type-approved for petrol operation.

## Function

- Stores LPG on board in a pressure tank and delivers it as a liquid to the engine bay.
- Limits filling to a safe level and cuts off gas flow on over-pressure, excess flow or excess temperature.
- Vaporises the liquid LPG and regulates its pressure to a stable low value for metering.
- Injects the correct quantity of gas into the intake to match engine demand.
- Switches automatically between petrol and LPG and controls the gas injectors from a dedicated electronic control unit.
- Keeps the LPG installation isolated and safe when the vehicle is parked or the engine is off.

## Main Parts

- LPG pressure tank with mounting brackets.
- Multivalve (fill limiter, excess-flow valve, pressure relief, solenoid shut-off, level sensor).
- Filler unit and fill line.
- Vaporiser/reducer with pressure regulation and coolant heating.
- Gas injectors and injector rail.
- Electronic control unit with wiring harness and changeover switch.
- Gas supply and return lines, filter and fittings.
