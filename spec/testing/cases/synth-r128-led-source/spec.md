# spec: LED Light Source

## Product Type

Replaceable LED light source — a single-function light source with one red LED and a standard cap, made to be fitted in the red rear signalling lamp of a passenger car and replaced in service.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car (the vehicle whose lamp it is fitted in).

## Function

- Emit red light with a defined luminous flux and angular distribution
- Run from the 12 V vehicle supply (rated 12 V, characterised at 13.5 V)
- Place the light-emitting area at a defined position relative to the cap
- Hold and contact the source in the lamp holder through a standard cap
- Remove LED heat through an integrated heat sink, up to a declared maximum operating temperature
- Carry identification marks on the cap

## Main Parts

- LED package (one red LED die, no phosphor conversion)
- Substrate (printed circuit board)
- Cap with contact pins
- Integrated heat sink
