# spec: M1 Front Bumper Assembly with Four Ultrasonic Parking Sensors and Two Front Fog Lamps

## Product Type

A front bumper assembly for passenger cars that combines the structural and cosmetic front-end cover with four ultrasonic parking sensors and two front fog lamps, providing low-speed obstacle detection and supplementary frontal illumination.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to the front end of a passenger car (up to eight seats) whose body structure and lighting installation are type-approved as a complete vehicle.

## Function

- Covers and closes the front end of the vehicle, absorbing low-energy impacts and hiding the front structure.
- Houses and positions four ultrasonic sensors that measure the distance to obstacles ahead and to the front corners.
- Provides the beam pattern for two front fog lamps to improve visibility in fog, heavy rain or falling snow.
- Carries the sensor and lamp wiring harness and connectors to the vehicle electrical system.
- Presents a defined outer surface to pedestrians and other road users through its shape and finish.

## Main Parts

- Bumper fascia (outer skin) with sensor bezels and lamp openings.
- Bumper energy absorber and cross-beam mounting brackets.
- Four ultrasonic parking sensors with mounting rings and seals.
- Two front fog lamp units (housing, reflector, lens, bulb or LED module).
- Wiring harness, connectors and sensor/lamp fasteners.
- Grille insert and lower air-deflector trim.
