# spec: L3 Road Motorcycle with Headlamp, Direction Indicators and Stop Lamp

## Product Type
A two-wheeled road motorcycle propelled by an internal combustion engine, fitted with a front headlamp, front and rear direction indicators and a rear stop lamp. It is a series-production vehicle intended for use on public roads, carrying one rider and one passenger.

## Vehicle Category
UNECE category L3 — a two-wheeled motorcycle; it is fitted with the lighting and light-signalling devices listed above and is type-approved as a complete vehicle.

## Function
- Provides road illumination ahead of the vehicle through the headlamp in low and high beam.
- Signals the rider's intended change of direction using the front and rear direction indicators.
- Signals braking to following traffic through the stop lamp.
- Provides a mounting and electrical interface for the lighting and signalling devices on the motorcycle.
- Carries the rider and passenger and transmits drive and braking forces to the road.

## Main Parts
- Headlamp assembly with filament or LED light source, reflector, lens and aim adjustment.
- Front and rear direction indicators with amber lens and flasher control.
- Stop lamp with red lens, operated by the brake control.
- Lighting switchgear and flasher relay/control unit.
- Wiring harness, connectors and fuses.
- Frame, front fork, handlebar and rear swingarm.
- Engine, transmission and final drive.
- Wheels and tyres, front and rear.
- Front and rear brakes.
- Fuel tank, seat and bodywork.
