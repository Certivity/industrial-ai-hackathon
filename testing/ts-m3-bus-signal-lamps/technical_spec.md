# spec: Intercity Bus Front/Rear Signalling Lamp and Retro-reflector Set (M3)

## Product Type

A matched set of front and rear light-signalling devices (position lamps, direction indicators, stop lamps, rear fog lamp, reversing lamp, end-outline marker lamps) and retro-reflectors for an intercity bus. They emit, reflect and signal the bus's presence, direction changes and braking to other road users.

## Vehicle Category

M3 — a bus with more than eight passenger seats plus the driver, exceeding 5 t, used for intercity service.

## Function

- Show the bus's front and rear position and width to other road users.
- Indicate turning and lane-change intentions via direction indicators.
- Signal braking via stop lamps and rear-end presence via the rear fog lamp.
- Illuminate the path behind when reversing.
- Return incident light to observers via retro-reflectors when lamps are off.
- Mark the bus's overall outline and extremities at the front and rear via end-outline marker lamps.

## Main Parts

- Front position (side) lamps, left and right.
- Front direction indicators, left and right.
- Rear position lamps, left and right.
- Rear direction indicators, left and right.
- Stop lamps, left and right.
- Rear fog lamp(s).
- Reversing lamp(s).
- End-outline marker lamps (front and rear).
- Rear retro-reflectors, left and right (non-triangular).
- Lamp housings, lenses, seals and mounting brackets; wiring harness connectors.
