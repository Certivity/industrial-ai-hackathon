# spec: Steering Lock with Electronic Immobiliser and Alarm for L3 Motorcycle

## Product Type

An integrated motorcycle anti-theft device combining a mechanical steering lock, an electronic engine immobiliser and a theft alarm, supplied as one unit for factory or retrofit fitment.

## Vehicle Category

L3 — a two-wheel motorcycle; the device is fitted to the steering column and the engine/ignition circuit of that vehicle.

## Function

- Locks the steering column mechanically to prevent the motorcycle being steered or pushed away when parked.
- Electronically verifies the rider's key/transponder and enables the engine start circuit only after successful authentication.
- Automatically immobilises the engine when the key is removed or the ignition is switched off.
- Detects unauthorised movement, tampering or battery disconnection and sounds an alarm.
- Signals alarm and arming status and can be armed/disarmed remotely.

## Main Parts

- Lock body with key barrel and locking bolt.
- Electric actuator/steering-lock release mechanism.
- Immobiliser control unit (transponder reader and engine cut-off relay).
- Alarm siren and motion/tilt sensor.
- Remote key fob transmitter and receiver module.
- Wiring harness and connectors.
