# spec: Frontal Impact Body Structure with Front Seat Restraint System

## Product Type

A passenger car body-in-white with front structural crash members and a front seat restraint system (seat belts, belt pre-tensioners, load limiters and airbag modules) that together absorb frontal impact energy and restrain the front occupants.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to passenger cars designed and constructed for the carriage of not more than eight passengers in addition to the driver.

## Function

- Absorb and dissipate kinetic energy in a frontal collision through controlled deformation of the front structure.
- Maintain survival space for the front occupants by limiting intrusion into the passenger compartment.
- Restrain front seat occupants with three-point seat belts, pre-tensioners and load limiters.
- Deploy frontal airbags to cushion the head and chest of the driver and front passenger.
- Manage deceleration of the occupant to reduce injury criteria (head, chest, femur).
- Provide compatible load paths so the restraint system works with the body structure.

## Main Parts

- Front longitudinal rails with crash boxes.
- Front bumper beam and energy-absorbing crash element.
- Subframe and engine cradle mounts.
- Dash panel cross-car beam and toe-board reinforcement.
- A-pillar and hinge-pillar reinforcements.
- Seat belt retractors with pre-tensioners and load limiters.
- Belt buckles and anchorages.
- Driver and front passenger frontal airbag modules.
- Airbag electronic control unit and crash sensors.
- Steering column energy-absorbing element.
