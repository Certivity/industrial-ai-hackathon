# spec: Hydraulic Service Brake System with ABS and Brake Assist (M1)

## Product Type

A hydraulically actuated service brake system for a passenger car that provides base braking, anti-lock control and emergency brake assistance through a single integrated hydraulic modulator and electronic control unit.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with up to nine seats including the driver, designed and built for carrying passengers and their luggage.

## Function

- Convert brake pedal force into hydraulic pressure that applies friction brakes at all four wheels.
- Distribute braking effort between front and rear axles to keep the car stable under normal and partial braking.
- Detect wheel lock-up and modulate pressure per wheel to preserve steering control and shorten stopping distance.
- Recognise an emergency pedal application and boost hydraulic pressure to maximum faster than the driver can, then release when the pedal is withdrawn.
- Provide a redundant, mechanically independent path to the front brakes so the car can still be stopped if the powered hydraulic circuit fails.
- Report faults to the driver through warning lamps and store diagnostic information for service.

## Main Parts

- Tandem brake master cylinder with reservoir and fluid level sensor.
- Vacuum or electro-hydraulic brake booster with integrated pedal travel and force sensing.
- Hydraulic modulator block containing pump, accumulators, inlet and outlet solenoid valves per wheel circuit.
- Electronic control unit for ABS and brake assist, mounted on or near the modulator.
- Four wheel-speed sensors and toothed tone rings.
- Brake pedal switch and brake assist pressure or travel sensor.
- Front and rear disc brakes with calipers, pads and flexible hoses.
- Steel brake lines to each wheel circuit.
- Instrument cluster warning lamps for ABS and brake system status.
