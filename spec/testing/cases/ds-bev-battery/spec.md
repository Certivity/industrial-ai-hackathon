# spec: High-Voltage Traction Battery Pack with Liquid Cooling and BMS

## Product Type
A rechargeable high-voltage lithium-ion traction battery pack with integrated liquid cooling circuit and battery management system (BMS), providing energy storage and delivery for the traction motor of a battery-electric vehicle.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — fitted to a battery-electric passenger car with no combustion engine, powered solely from the traction battery.

## Function
- Store electrical energy and supply high-voltage DC power to the traction inverter and motor
- Manage charge and discharge current, voltage and state of charge/health through the BMS
- Keep cells within safe operating temperature via a liquid cooling/heating circuit
- Isolate the high-voltage system on fault, crash signal or service disconnect
- Monitor cell voltages and temperatures and report status to the vehicle controller
- Balance cells and protect against overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent and overtemperature
- Communicate with the charger and vehicle network during charging and driving

## Main Parts
- Battery enclosure (tray and cover) with mounting and crash structure
- Cell modules arranged in packs
- Liquid cooling plate / cold plate and coolant manifold with inlet/outlet ports
- High-voltage busbars, contactors and fuse
- Service disconnect unit (manual service disconnect)
- Battery management system controller, cell monitoring boards and harness
- Coolant temperature and flow sensors, cell temperature sensors
- High-voltage connectors and sealed cable feedthroughs
- Ventilation / pressure-equalisation and venting device
