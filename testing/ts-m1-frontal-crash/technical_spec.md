# spec: Frontal impact protection structure and front-seat restraint system

## Product Type

A welded steel front-end structure combined with a front-seat restraint system (seat belts with pretensioners and load limiters, airbag modules and seat frames) that together absorb a frontal crash and hold the front occupants in place.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — installed in a passenger car with at least four wheels, designed and built for the carriage of up to eight occupants.

## Function

- Absorb and dissipate kinetic energy of a frontal impact in a controlled deformation of the front structure
- Limit the intrusion into the passenger compartment so the survival space for the front occupants is kept
- Restrain the front occupants through the belts, pretensioners and airbags to reduce the loading on the head, neck, chest and pelvis
- Distribute the crash loads into the body-in-white and the seat frames so the restraint system works as designed

## Main Parts

- Front longitudinal members, crash boxes and bumper beam
- Front cross members, suspension towers and subframe
- Front seat frames with belt anchorages
- Front seat belts with retractors, pretensioners and load limiters
- Front airbag modules (driver and passenger) and their control unit
- Steering column with energy-absorbing deformation element
