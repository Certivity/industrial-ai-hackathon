# spec: Instrument Cluster with Speedometer and Odometer (M1)

## Product Type

An electronic instrument cluster that presents vehicle speed and accumulated distance to the driver, plus supplementary tell-tales and gauges, on a digital display driven by a microcontroller.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with a maximum of eight seats in addition to the driver's seat.

## Function

- Displays instantaneous vehicle speed derived from wheel-speed or driveline sensors.
- Displays total distance travelled (odometer) and, where fitted, trip distance.
- Shows tell-tales and status indicators for functions such as lighting, direction indicators, brakes, and driver assistance.
- Provides illumination for night-time readability and dimming control.
- Stores and reports diagnostic and configuration data to the vehicle network.

## Main Parts

- Housing and front bezel.
- Printed circuit board with microcontroller and power supply.
- Display panel (TFT or segment LCD).
- Speed and distance signal interface (CAN transceiver / sensor input).
- Tell-tale LEDs and backlight driver.
- Connector harness and mounting brackets.
