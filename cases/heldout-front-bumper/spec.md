# spec: Bumper

## Product Type

Bumper (front) — exterior body/trim module combining fascia, energy-management structure, and integrated sensing, lighting, and electronics.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car (UNECE / EU 2018/858).

## Function

- Styling / exterior appearance
- Impact & energy management (crash and pedestrian protection)
- Sensing & driver assistance carrier (radar, LIDAR, camera, PDC, thermal)
- Lighting integration (signalling, decorative, functional)
- Thermal / airflow management
- Feature integration (towing cover, keyless-entry aerial, EV charge port)

## Main Parts

- Cover part (fascia)
- (Radiator) Grill
- Absorber
- Lower stiffener (underrun protection)
- License plate area / holder
- Towing device cover
- Camera
- Radar sensor
- Radar / LIDAR sensor (incl. heating)
- PDC sensor
- Pedestrian impact sensors
- Temperature / thermal sensor
- Lighting — decorative
- (Side) Lighting — signalling
- Aerial (keyless entry)
- "Fuel filler cap" (for EVs)
