# spec: Retro-Reflective Conspicuity Marking Tape (Side and Rear, Heavy Trailer O4)

## Product Type

A self-adhesive retro-reflective marking tape supplied in rolls, applied to the sides and rear of a heavy trailer to make the vehicle's outline conspicuous in the dark when illuminated by other road users' lights. It is a passive optical component; it has no electrical connection.

## Vehicle Category

O4 — a heavy full trailer or semi-trailer with a maximum mass above 10 t, intended for towing by an N-category motor vehicle; the tape is fitted along the side and rear contours of that trailer.

## Function

- Returns incident light back to its source so the trailer's outline is seen early by approaching drivers.
- Marks the side length and rear width and height of the trailer, showing its extent and shape.
- Indicates the presence of a large vehicle in low-light and unlit conditions.
- Keeps a stable, bright, colour-coded signal under weather, dirt and wash exposure.
- Stays bonded to painted, anodised or galvanised trailer surfaces over the vehicle's life.

## Main Parts

- Retro-reflective sheeting with micro-prismatic or glass-bead optics.
- Pressure-sensitive adhesive backing.
- Removable liner protecting the adhesive until application.
- Optional surface primer / adhesion promoter for difficult substrates.
- Segment/orientation marking and cut lines on the liner for correct application.
