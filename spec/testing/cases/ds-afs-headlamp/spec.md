# spec: Adaptive Front Lighting System with Cornering Light and Motorway Beam

## Product Type

A headlamp system that automatically adapts the low beam pattern to driving conditions—including speed‑dependent motorway beam and steering‑linked cornering light—to improve forward visibility without dazzling other road users.

## Vehicle Category

M1 – installed as original equipment on a passenger car.

## Function

- Provides a bend‑adaptive low beam that swivels horizontally with steering angle and vehicle speed.
- Activates a dedicated cornering light below a set speed to illuminate the inside of tight turns.
- Raises the cut‑off and extends the beam range at motorway speeds to improve long‑distance visibility.
- Maintains a defined cut‑off to avoid glare to oncoming traffic and preceding vehicles.
- Switches between lighting modes automatically based on vehicle speed, steering angle and yaw rate.

## Main Parts

- Two headlamp units with integrated projection modules and swivel actuators.
- LED light sources for low beam, high beam and cornering light functions.
- Electronic control unit (ECU) for adaptive lighting, receiving vehicle speed and steering signals.
- Stepper or DC motors for horizontal swivelling of the projection modules.
- Additional LED cornering lamp integrated into each headlamp housing.
- Leveling actuators to maintain headlamp aim regardless of load.
