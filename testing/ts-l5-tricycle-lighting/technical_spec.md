# spec: L5e Rear Lighting and Retro-Reflector Assembly

## Product Type

A rear lighting and retro-reflector assembly for a motorised three-wheel tricycle, providing the rear position, stop, direction indicator and rear registration plate illumination functions plus rear retro-reflectors, delivered as an integrated unit or as a set of matched lamps for the vehicle rear.

## Vehicle Category

L5e (three-wheeled power-driven vehicle, motorised tricycle); fitted to the rear body of the tricycle and wired into its 12 V lighting electrical system.

## Function

- Show the vehicle's presence from behind with rear position lamps.
- Signal braking to following traffic with stop lamps.
- Indicate turning and directional changes with direction indicators.
- Illuminate the rear registration plate.
- Return light towards the observer with fixed rear retro-reflectors.

## Main Parts

- Rear position lamp (red, LED or filament light source)
- Stop lamp (red, combined with position lamp)
- Direction indicator lamps, rear (amber)
- Rear registration plate lamp (white)
- Rear retro-reflectors, non-triangular (red)
- Housing, lens, gasket and mounting bracket
- Connector harness and electronic control/drive board
