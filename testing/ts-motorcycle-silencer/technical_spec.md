# spec: Replacement Exhaust Silencing System for L3 Motorcycle

## Product Type
A replacement exhaust silencing system (muffler/silencer and connecting pipe) for a motorcycle, designed to reduce exhaust noise to a level comparable to the original system while maintaining engine performance and exhaust flow.

## Vehicle Category
UNECE L3: a two-wheeled motorcycle with an engine capacity exceeding 50 cm³ or a maximum design speed exceeding 50 km/h.

## Function
- Attenuates exhaust noise to meet permissible sound levels.
- Routes exhaust gases from the engine to the atmosphere.
- Provides a mounting interface to the motorcycle frame or engine.
- Maintains exhaust backpressure within acceptable limits for engine operation.

## Main Parts
- Silencer body (muffler canister).
- Inlet pipe and connecting pipe.
- Internal baffles, perforated tubes and sound-absorbing material.
- End cap and outlet.
- Mounting brackets and clamps (where provided).
