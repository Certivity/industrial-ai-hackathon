# spec: Radar Advanced Emergency Braking System for N3 Heavy Goods Vehicles

## Product Type

A forward-looking radar-based advanced emergency braking system that detects vehicles and obstacles ahead and automatically applies the service brakes to avoid or mitigate rear-end collisions. It provides forward collision warning and autonomous braking as an integrated active-safety function of the vehicle.

## Vehicle Category

N3 — fitted to a heavy goods vehicle (rigid truck) with a maximum gross mass above 12 t, 4x2 or 6x2 configuration.

## Function

- Measures range and closing speed to obstacles ahead using a 77 GHz forward radar sensor.
- Warns the driver with visual and audible alerts before an impending collision.
- Calculates a time-to-collision and initiates automatic service-brake application when the driver does not react.
- Applies staged braking to first reduce impact severity and then bring the vehicle to a standstill where possible.
- Suppresses braking when the detected object is judged not to be a real collision threat.
- Monitors its own sensor and actuator health and signals a fault to the driver.
- Allows the driver to switch the braking intervention off but cannot be permanently disabled by default.

## Main Parts

- Forward radar sensor module (77 GHz, housed behind the front bumper).
- Electronic control unit running the object-tracking and collision-decision software.
- Interface and wiring harness to the vehicle brake system and power supply.
- Driver warning display, with acoustic warning device.
- Speed and steering-angle inputs tapped from the vehicle's on-board sensors.
- Brake actuator interface to the air-brake system via the vehicle's electronic braking system.
