# spec: Electric Power Steering with Corrective Steering Function

## Product Type

An electric power steering system for a light passenger car: a steering column with an electric motor-driven assist unit that provides power assistance and a corrective steering function acting on the column. It does not itself steer autonomously; it assists and corrects the driver's steering input.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to passenger cars carrying up to eight occupants in addition to the driver.

## Function

- Provides electric power assistance to the driver's steering effort, scaled with speed and steering torque.
- Applies a corrective steering function: small, limited steering-angle corrections via the column to improve directional stability.
- Detects driver override and yields control immediately when the driver turns the wheel.
- Reports system status and faults; reverts to manual (fail-safe or fail-degraded) steering if assist is lost.
- Receives vehicle signals (speed, yaw rate, steering angle, torque) over the vehicle network.

## Main Parts

- Steering column with integrated electric assist motor and gear reducer.
- Steering torque and steering-angle sensors.
- Electronic control unit (ECU) with embedded software for assist and correction logic.
- Electric power and signal wiring harness with connectors.
- Steering mechanism (rack-and-pinion steering gear) driven by the column.
