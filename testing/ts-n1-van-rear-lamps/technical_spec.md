# spec: Rear Fog Lamp and Reversing Lamp Assembly for N1 Light Delivery Van

## Product Type

A combined rear lighting unit carrying a rear fog lamp and a reversing lamp, mounted on the rear of a light delivery van to warn following traffic of reduced visibility and to provide white light and an audible/visual aid when reversing.

## Vehicle Category

N1 — light goods vehicle with a maximum mass not exceeding 3.5 t; fitted as original rear lighting equipment on the van's rear body panel.

## Function

- Rear fog lamp: emits a red high-intensity signal to the rear when visibility is seriously reduced.
- Reversing lamp: emits white light to illuminate the area behind and signal reverse gear engagement.
- Provides mounting, sealing and electrical connection for both functions in one housing.

## Main Parts

- Lamp housing with red (fog) and clear (reversing) optical compartments.
- Red lens and clear lens, each with the required photometric pattern.
- Light source (filament bulbs or LED modules) for each function.
- Reflector optics for each compartment.
- Gasket and rear cover for sealing against water and dust.
- Connector and wiring harness pigtail.
- Mounting bracket and fasteners.
