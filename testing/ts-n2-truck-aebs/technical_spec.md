# spec: AEBS-M2 medium truck advanced emergency braking system

## Product Type

An advanced emergency braking system (AEBS) for a medium truck (N2) that warns the driver of an impending collision and automatically applies the service brakes to reduce or avoid a rear-end impact with a moving or stationary vehicle ahead.

## Vehicle Category

N2 — a medium goods vehicle with a maximum mass between 3.5 t and 12 t, fitted with a service braking system and a driver-operated warning interface.

## Function

- Detect vehicles ahead in the truck's own lane using forward-looking radar and/or camera sensors.
- Estimate time-to-collision and identify an imminent rear-end collision risk.
- Issue a driver warning (acoustic and/or haptic) before emergency intervention.
- Provide a forward collision warning in the speed range defined for the vehicle class.
- Command emergency braking through the service brake system (and request torque reduction where present) to slow the truck.
- Continue braking/monitor after impact to limit secondary effects where the system is so designed.
- Report system status and faults to the driver and disable intervention when the system is degraded.

## Main Parts

- Forward-looking radar module (76–81 GHz) mounted in the front bumper/grille.
- Forward-looking camera module behind the windscreen or in the front fascia.
- AEBS electronic control unit (ECU) with fusion and decision logic.
- Brake actuator interface to the service brake system (pneumatic or hydraulic modulator / EBS).
- Vehicle motion and driver input sensors (wheel-speed, yaw-rate, steering angle, brake pedal).
- Driver warning devices (instrument cluster lamp, buzzer, haptic seat/belt where fitted).
- Wiring harness, connectors and power supply with EMC filtering.
