# spec: Active Pedestrian Protection Front-End Structure

## Product Type

A front-end structure for a passenger car that absorbs energy in a collision and includes a deployable active bonnet to reduce injury to pedestrians.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — the vehicle is a passenger car with at least four wheels and no more than eight seats in addition to the driver.

## Function

- Absorbs impact energy in low-speed frontal collisions via an energy-absorbing bumper.
- Deploys the active bonnet to create a deformation space between the bonnet and hard under-hood components.
- Provides a pedestrian-friendly front surface with controlled stiffness and geometry.

## Main Parts

- Energy-absorbing bumper beam and crash boxes.
- Bumper fascia with pedestrian-friendly design.
- Active bonnet with pyrotechnic or spring actuators.
- Bonnet hinges and latch with deployment kinematics.
- Pedestrian impact sensors (accelerometers/pressure sensors) and ECU.
