# spec: Replacement Catalytic Converter for Petrol M1 Passenger Cars

## Product Type

A replacement catalytic converter assembly that replaces the original equipment converter in the exhaust line of a petrol passenger car, converting carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons and nitrogen oxides in the exhaust gas into less harmful compounds.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to passenger cars carrying up to eight seated occupants in addition to the driver, powered by a positive-ignition (petrol) engine.

## Function

- Oxidises carbon monoxide and unburnt hydrocarbons in the exhaust gas.
- Reduces nitrogen oxides in the exhaust gas under controlled air/fuel conditions.
- Matches the back-pressure and flow characteristics of the original converter so engine output and emissions control behave comparably.
- Provides a durable, directly interchangeable replacement that does not degrade the vehicle's approved emissions performance.

## Main Parts

- Metallic substrate carrier (honeycomb monolith) or ceramic substrate with washcoat.
- Catalytic washcoat carrying platinum, palladium and rhodium.
- Stainless-steel outer shell and inlet/outlet cones.
- Mounting flanges, gaskets and pipe stubs matching the original interface.
- Thermal insulation mat between substrate and shell.
- Sensor bosses (upstream and downstream lambda sensor ports) where the original part has them.
