# spec: TempoMatic T125 Compact Spare Wheel and Tyre Unit

## Product Type

A temporary-use spare wheel and tyre assembly supplied as emergency equipment for a passenger car. It is a narrow-space-saving wheel and inflated tyre unit that lets the car be driven to a service point after a puncture.

## Vehicle Category

M1; the unit is carried on board and mounted only on a passenger car, on the axle designated by its manufacturer.

## Function

- Replaces a punctured road wheel so the car can be driven at reduced speed to a workshop.
- Supports the vehicle mass and transmits drive, braking and steering forces for a limited distance.
- Stows compactly in the luggage compartment, floor well or under-floor recess.
- Provides a visible and documented speed and distance limit so the driver does not exceed safe use conditions.

## Main Parts

- Steel rim of reduced width with hub-centered bolt holes and matching offset.
- Inflated temporary-use tyre, narrower and smaller in section height than the road tyres.
- Rubber valve with a pressure-retaining cap.
- Warning label with the maximum speed and maximum distance of use, fixed on the wheel or the tyre sidewall.
- Retaining hardware, hub-cap cover or fixing kit for stowing the unit in the vehicle.
