# spec: Front Seat Safety-Belt Reminder System (SBR)

## Product Type

An electronic safety-belt reminder system for a passenger car that illuminates a warning lamp and sounds an acoustic warning when front-seat occupants are unbuckled while the vehicle is in use.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with two front seating positions, powered by an internal-combustion or electric powertrain, using standard buckled/unbuckled seat-belt switches.

## Function

- Detect the use state (buckled or unbuckled) of each front seat via belt-buckle switches.
- Detect occupancy of the front passenger seat via a seat-occupancy sensor.
- Illuminate a telltale warning lamp in the instrument cluster when a front occupant is unbuckled and the vehicle is powered/in motion.
- Add a supplementary visual signal and a two-stage acoustic warning (low then high) sequence per the standard reminder logic.
- Suppress the reminder when the belt is buckled or the seat is unoccupied.
- Self-check the lamp and buzzer at power-on.

## Main Parts

- Reminder control unit (logic and driver stage).
- Instrument-cluster warning lamp (belt telltale).
- Acoustic warning device (piezo buzzer or audio amplifier and speaker).
- Front seat-belt buckle switches (Hall or contact type).
- Front passenger seat-occupancy sensor (pressure mat or film sensor).
- Wiring harness and connectors.
