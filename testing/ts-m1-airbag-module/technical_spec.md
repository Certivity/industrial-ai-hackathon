# spec: Replacement airbag module with integrated steering wheel (driver side, M1)

## Product Type

A complete driver-side steering wheel assembly with an integrated frontal airbag module and its inflator, supplied as a like-for-like replacement for the original unit on a passenger car. It restores the original restraint function and gives the driver a grip, horn and steering input.

## Vehicle Category

M1 passenger car; fitted to the driver position of a vehicle already type-approved with a driver frontal airbag and a steering wheel of the same interface.

## Function

- Transmit steering input from the driver to the steering column.
- Provide a deformable, energy-absorbing airbag cover facing the driver.
- Deploy the frontal airbag in a defined frontal impact to cushion the driver's head and chest.
- Absorb deployment energy through a controlled inflator and folded bag.
- Carry the horn switch and any steering-wheel-mounted controls of the original unit.
- Present no sharp or projecting edges to the driver in normal use or during deployment.

## Main Parts

- Steering wheel rim and spokes (metal core with soft cover).
- Hub with splined or keyed connection to the steering column.
- Airbag module housing and folded airbag cushion.
- Pyrotechnic inflator (gas generator) with electrical connector.
- Tear-seam airbag cover / driver-facing pad.
- Horn switch and wiring harness.
- Mounting bolts and locating features.
