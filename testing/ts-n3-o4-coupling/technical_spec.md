# spec: Close-Coupling Device for Tractor-Semi-Trailer Combinations

## Product Type

A mechanical coupling that connects a tractor unit semi-trailer kingpin to the tractor unit's fifth wheel, allowing articulation while transferring towing and braking forces.

## Vehicle Category

N3 tractor unit coupled to an O4 semi-trailer (over 12 000 kg gross vehicle mass).

## Function

- Provides a secure, articulated connection between the tractor unit and the semi-trailer.
- Transfers longitudinal (tractive and braking) forces from the semi-trailer to the tractor.
- Transfers vertical load from the semi-trailer to the tractor unit's fifth wheel.
- Permits horizontal articulation and pitch between tractor and semi-trailer during normal operation.
- Allows coupling and uncoupling of the semi-trailer by the operator.

## Main Parts

- Coupling plate (mounting interface to the semi-trailer).
- Kingpin.
- Locking mechanism (jaw or latch).
- Release handle / operating lever.
- Wear ring / bearing surface.
- Mounting bolts and fasteners.
