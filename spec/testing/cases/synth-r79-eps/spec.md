# spec: Electric Power Steering

## Product Type

Electric power-assisted steering (EPS) of a passenger car — a column-mounted electric motor and control unit that add assist torque to the driver's input while the mechanical link from steering wheel to road wheels stays in place.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car with an internal combustion engine and a 12 V network; no coupling for charging a traction battery; the front wheels are steered.

## Function

- Steer the front wheels from the steering wheel through column, intermediate shaft and rack
- Add assist torque from an electric motor, controlled by an electronic control unit from torque and vehicle-speed signals
- Self-centre after a turn and run straight at speed without correction
- Detect faults in the electronic control and warn the driver (optical telltale and acoustic chime)
- Keep the vehicle steerable, at a higher effort, when the assist fails
- Adjust the tie-rod length for toe setting
- Allow inspection of the steering and its electronics in service without dismantling

## Main Parts

- Steering wheel and column
- Electric assist motor
- Electronic control unit (ECU) with torque sensor
- Steering rack and adjustable tie rods
- Telltale and warning chime
