# spec: plug-in hybrid electric passenger car with type-approved electric range, energy consumption and CO2 emissions

## Product Type

A plug-in hybrid electric passenger car that combines a spark-ignition engine with a rechargeable traction battery and electric motor to drive the wheels, and that can be recharged from an external electric supply. Its electric range, electric energy consumption and CO2 emissions are declared and type-approved on the basis of chassis-dynamometer test results.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — a passenger car with no more than eight seats in addition to the driver's seat, fitted with a plug-in hybrid electric powertrain and a high-voltage traction battery.

## Function

- Drives the vehicle using the electric motor alone, the combustion engine alone, or both together, depending on the operating mode.
- Stores electric energy in a high-voltage traction battery that can be recharged from an external AC or DC supply.
- Measures the traction battery current and voltage during the type-approval tests so that the electric energy used and the electric range can be determined.
- Uses the measured battery energy together with other test data to determine electric energy consumption and CO2 emissions.
- Maintains battery state of charge and thermal condition within safe operating limits while driving and charging.

## Main Parts

- Spark-ignition internal combustion engine with exhaust after-treatment.
- Electric traction motor/generator.
- High-voltage traction battery (REESS) with integrated battery management system.
- Current sensor and voltage sensor on the traction battery, providing signals used during type-approval testing.
- Power electronics: inverter, DC/DC converter and on-board charger.
- Hybrid control unit managing the power split between engine and motor.
- Fuel tank and fuel supply system.
- Exhaust emission control system.
- Charge port and charge cable interface.
- Instrument cluster showing electric range and energy consumption.
