# spec: Combined Speedometer and Odometer Unit for Light Commercial Vehicles

## Product Type

An electronic instrument cluster module that displays the vehicle's road speed to the driver and records the total distance travelled. It is a self-contained unit with a display, a microcontroller, and a signal input from the vehicle's driveline speed sensor.

## Vehicle Category

N1 — light commercial vehicle (car-derived van or light goods vehicle), fitted as original equipment in the dashboard.

## Function

- Shows the instantaneous road speed on an analogue dial or digital display, calibrated so it never under-reads.
- Accumulates and stores the total distance travelled (odometer) in non-volatile memory.
- Optionally shows a resettable trip distance.
- Accepts a speed signal from a wheel/gearbox sensor or wheel-speed bus and converts it to indicated speed and distance.
- Self-checks the display and sensor input at power-on and flags a fault.

## Main Parts

- Housing and front bezel with the dial face and pointer (or LCD panel).
- Printed circuit board with microcontroller, signal-conditioning circuit and non-volatile memory.
- Stepper motor or display driver for the speed indication.
- Connector and wiring harness interface to the vehicle speed signal and power supply.
- Backlighting and illumination control.
