# spec: Vehicle Alarm System (VAS-1)

## Product Type

A factory-fitted electronic theft-deterrent and alarm system for a passenger car. It monitors door contacts, the cabin interior and the vehicle supply, and raises an audible and visual alarm when unauthorised entry or interference is detected.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with a 12 V electrical system, four side doors plus tailgate, and a factory body-control network.

## Function

- Arms and disarms the alarm under control of the central locking / remote key.
- Detects opening of any monitored door, bonnet or tailgate via contact switches.
- Detects movement inside the cabin using an ultrasonic sensor pair while armed.
- Detects supply interruption, e.g. battery disconnection or cutting of the siren wiring.
- Sounds the siren and flashes the direction indicators when an alarm is triggered.
- Limits each alarm cycle in duration and number of re-triggers to avoid nuisance.
- Self-checks sensors and wiring at arming and reports a fault by a short diagnostic signal.
- Reports status and trigger history to the vehicle diagnostic interface.

## Main Parts

- Alarm control unit (electronic module with microcontroller and immobiliser interface).
- Ultrasonic interior sensor unit (emitter/receiver pair, ceiling or pillar mounted).
- Door, bonnet and tailgate contact switches (pin type).
- Siren with self-powered backup battery.
- Status indicator LED and interior push-button override.
- Wiring harness and connectors with tamper-protected routing.
