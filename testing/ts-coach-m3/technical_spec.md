# spec: Intercity Coach Passenger Compartment System — Seats, Emergency Exits, Interior Fittings and Heating

## Product Type

A complete intercity coach body-and-interior assembly for carriage of seated passengers only, comprising the passenger seat rows, the emergency exits and their markings, the interior fittings (handrails, partition, luggage racks, aisle trim) and the passenger-compartment heating system, delivered as a fitted vehicle.

## Vehicle Category

M3, Class III (intercity coach, 50 seats, no standing passengers permitted); fitted to a rigid two-axle coach with a fully enclosed heated passenger saloon.

## Function

- Seat and restrain up to 50 passengers in forward-facing rows with defined spacing and anchorage strength.
- Provide a defined number of service doors and emergency exits (doors, windows, roof hatches) with correct dimensions, operation, access and escape routes.
- Mark and illuminate exits and escape routes so they are findable in darkness or smoke.
- Keep the saloon interior free of sharp edges, projections and entrapment hazards and provide handholds along the aisle and at doorways.
- Heat and distribute air through the passenger compartment to maintain occupant thermal comfort across the operating temperature range.
- Withstand seat, handrail and exit loads without detaching or blocking escape paths.

## Main Parts

- Passenger seat frames, cushions, backrests, head restraints and anchorage rails.
- Service doors, emergency doors, emergency windows (break-glass), roof emergency hatches.
- Exit markings, emergency lighting and exit pictograms.
- Interior handrails, grab poles, luggage racks, aisle and step trim, partition walls.
- Heating unit (coolant-fed heat exchanger with blower), ducting, floor convectors, controls and thermostat.
- Electrical harness, connectors and electronic control module for heating and emergency lighting.
