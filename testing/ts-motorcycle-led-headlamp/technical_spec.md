# spec: Motorcycle LED Headlamp (Main and Dipped Beam)

## Product Type

A single sealed LED headlamp unit that provides both a dipped (low) beam and a main (high) beam for a motorcycle. It is a complete lighting device combining the LED light sources, their control electronics and the optical assembly in one housing.

## Vehicle Category

L3e – fitted to a two-wheel motorcycle (L3e) as its sole or primary forward road-illumination device.

## Function

- Emit a dipped (low) beam with a defined cut-off so as not to dazzle oncoming traffic.
- Emit a main (high) beam for full-distance road illumination.
- Switch between dipped and main beam (and main-beam signalling/flashing) on driver command.
- Provide a stable light output and beam pattern independent of the supply voltage over the operating range.
- Tolerate the electrical environment of the motorcycle without emitting or being disturbed by electromagnetic interference.

## Main Parts

- One-piece housing and lens (sealed optical unit).
- LED light-source array for dipped beam.
- LED light-source array for main beam.
- LED driver / control electronics (constant-current, beam switching).
- Reflector / projection optics and beam-shaping shield giving the dipped-beam cut-off.
- Heat sink / thermal management for the LEDs.
- Connector/harness interface.
