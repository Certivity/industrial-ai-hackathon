# spec: Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System with Exterior Sound Emission Control for Battery Electric Passenger Car

## Product Type

An electronic acoustic vehicle alerting system (AVAS) that generates a synthetic warning sound at low vehicle speeds and manages the overall exterior sound emission of a battery electric passenger car.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a battery electric passenger car with no internal combustion engine, so an artificial alerting sound is required at low speeds.

## Function

- Emit an audible alerting sound when the vehicle travels forward or in reverse below the activation speed threshold so pedestrians can detect it.
- Raise the sound level and/or pitch as vehicle speed increases, up to the deactivation speed.
- Suppress the alerting sound above the threshold speed where tyre/road noise becomes sufficient.
- Provide a manual pause/disable function for stationary operation.
- Detect fault conditions in the sound generator and signal them to the driver.
- Keep the total exterior sound emission within the permitted limits for the vehicle.

## Main Parts

- Sound generator/controller unit (microprocessor with signal synthesis).
- Vehicle speed and direction input (CAN bus from powertrain/ABS).
- External loudspeaker(s) mounted in the front/engine-bay area.
- Driver pause switch and indicator.
- Fault detection and driver warning output.
- Housing, wiring harness and connectors.
