# spec: Rear Impact Attenuation Structure with Integrated Fuel Tank (M1)

## Product Type

A welded steel rear body structure enclosing the fuel tank, mounted to the rear floor and longitudinal rails of a passenger car; it absorbs and distributes rear-end collision energy while protecting the tank from deformation and leakage.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — fitted to a passenger car with a rear-mounted fuel tank between the rear longitudinal members.

## Function

- Absorb and dissipate kinetic energy of a rear-end impact through controlled crush of the rear rails and beam.
- Limit intrusion into the fuel tank and occupant compartment at the rear.
- Maintain fuel tank integrity (no rupture, no leakage) after a rear impact.
- Provide mounting and restraint for the fuel tank and rear bumper crossbeam.
- Distribute rear impact loads into the vehicle body-in-white.

## Main Parts

- Rear bumper crossbeam with crash boxes.
- Left and right rear longitudinal crash rails.
- Tank protection shield / skid plate.
- Fuel tank (steel or multilayer plastic) with filler neck and venting.
- Tank mounting straps and brackets.
- Rear floor cross-member and closing panel.
